# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome newcomers and different levels of experience. Debate ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile contact options; do not publish private details in an issue. Decisions should be proportionate and explained where privacy allows.
